Questions people ask first

What should I do first?

Shut off the water if you can do it safely, and keep clear of wet areas near outlets. Take photos before you move anything, report it to your insurance company, then call us.

How long does drying take?

It depends on how much water there was and what got wet. We take moisture readings as we go and tell you where it stands.

Does insurance cover water damage?

That depends on your policy and what caused it, and it is your insurer's decision. We photograph the damage and write the estimate so you have what they ask for.

Do you rebuild after the drying?

Yes. Drywall, flooring, trim and paint, so you are not left looking for a second contractor.
